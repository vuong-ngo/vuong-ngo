### 🪐 A Few Words

I wouldn't call myself an engineer yet. I'm a student learning to listen to silent systems — a hung process, a packet that lost its way, a container that dies without explanation. Every bug is a question, and I'm learning to answer them through a terminal more than through words.

- 🎓 Studying **System Engineering** — where stability is built from things you can't see
- 🛠️ Spending my time on **Linux, networking, and infrastructure** — the foundations nobody notices but everyone needs
- 🌱 Currently going deeper into **Docker** and **Networking**, one layer at a time, like learning a new language
- 💬 Happy to talk about **Bash, Python, C++, Java** — and why code runs fine at 2 AM but breaks the next morning
- 📫 Find me at **vuongngo.dev@gmail.com**, if your system has gone suspiciously quiet too
